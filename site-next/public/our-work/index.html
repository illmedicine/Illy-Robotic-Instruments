TidyLocal — Your Modern Local Cleaning Platform

Book it. Track it. Trust it. Total control over scope and cost.

TidyLocal is a modern, local cleaning platform that brings full transparency to hiring a cleaner. An app-based, granular booking flow lets clients define exactly what they want cleaned — room by room — with real-time, upfront pricing. Qualified local cleaners (TLCs) are matched instantly, funds are held in escrow, and payment only releases when the job is rated complete.

How It Works

Four steps down the line

1

Detailed & Upfront Booking

Users go beyond a generic "clean." A "dirtiness" slider, room count and size selectors, and a per-room checkbox list (sweep, mop, deep-clean the fridge or oven…) determine the price in real time — fully transparent. Booking time also affects cost (immediate service vs. scheduled ahead).

2

Rapid, Qualified Cleaner Matching

The request is instantly routed to the TidyLocal Job Board. Qualified local cleaners (TLCs) get alerted, each with a full profile — rating score, jobs completed, availability, transportation verification, and client reviews — so clients match with trusted individuals.

3

Secure Escrow Payment

When a cleaner accepts, an in-app instant messenger coordinates the details. The client pre-pays at booking and funds are held securely in escrow — no money changes hands at the door, keeping the exchange fair and safe for both sides.

4

Quality & Accountability

On completion, the client marks the job done and leaves a rating, instantly releasing the escrowed funds. The visible rating system rewards thorough, timely work and builds a trusted community; consistent poor reviews (or a "don't send again") carry real consequences.

In essence, TidyLocal gives clients total control over the scope and cost of their cleaning while giving qualified cleaners a steady stream of jobs and a secure, guaranteed payment process.

Investment Approach & Strategy

The NYC Pilot — a path to guaranteed payback

Launch strategy is contingent upon securing a $100,000 seed loan for the New York City pilot. To show investors exactly when the loan is repaid, we calculate the maximum capacity of a 5-person W2 team.

NYC Competitive Pricing Strategy

A standard professional home cleaning in NYC typically runs $168–$298 (up to ~$80/hr; a 1,600 sq ft home costs ~$160–$320). To aggressively capture share while keeping gig-worker pay attractive, TidyLocal institutes a $160 flat rate for a standard residential cleaning (~3 hours of labor).

Investor Takeaway

The W2 strategy isn't just about quality control — it's a massive revenue accelerator. Because TidyLocal captures 100% of the W2 booking fees, the initial 5 direct hires generate $104,000 on their own in the first 90 days. Even in the worst-case "Low Volume" scenario (Tier 1), the W2 employees effectively pay back the entire $100,000 loan principal independently.
