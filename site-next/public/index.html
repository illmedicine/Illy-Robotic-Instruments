Flagship · Echo Vue

Room intelligence from the WiFi you already have.

Echo Vue is IoT intelligence and automation for commercial spaces and homes. A small bridge on the wall turns existing WiFi signals into anonymised occupancy counts, with no camera, no microphone and no speaker.

No camera, no images. There is no camera input in the product at all. Occupancy was the goal, not identity.

Public areas only. Hallways, lobbies, pools and laundries. Sensing zones named like a room, suite or bathroom are rejected.

Estimates say so. Every figure carries a likely range and a confidence level. With no usable signal, the screen says so instead of guessing.

Short, aggregated memory. History is anonymous per-minute counts, kept 90 days, with no raw radio data and no identities.
